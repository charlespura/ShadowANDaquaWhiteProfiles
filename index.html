Our Story

Two very different dogs, one tight-knit pack

Shadow, the elder of the two at five years old, is the calm, dignified one — a small brown Shih Tzu who supervises the household with an air of quiet authority. Aqua, three years younger, is the energetic little brother — a pure white Aspin who never met a moment that couldn't use more running around.

They both live at home in Bagong Pag-asa, Gate 3, Bliss, and despite their differences in breed, age, and size, they share the exact same two loves in life: a good bowl of dog food with kanin, and playtime that goes on for hours.
